## Connect financial and commercial performance.

This report gives managers one place to see how products and sales teams are performing, where customers could be buying more, and what happens to the numbers under different scenarios. It runs on synthetic data, so feel free to click around.

## Put questions in context.

The scope starts with four questions, one per area of the report:

- **Financial analysis.** Performance by product, area and period, to find the most profitable lines and the costs worth reviewing.
- **Sales team analysis.** Compares salespeople and teams, so it’s clear who is driving results and who needs support.
- **Financial simulation.** What-if scenarios to test a decision against the numbers before making it.
- **Cross-sell.** Which products are bought together, and which customers are good candidates for a complementary product.

Every page shares the same filters, so you can go from the whole company down to a single product or salesperson without losing context.

<Note>The data is synthetic and the report shows no client information. A page-by-page breakdown of the analysis and the modelling decisions behind it is on the way.</Note>
